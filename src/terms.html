2. What the add-in does and doesn't do

The add-in shows the email authentication results (DMARC, SPF, DKIM, ARC) that your mail server recorded in a message's headers, and points out signs of forgery. It is an aid to your judgement, not a guarantee. A “Verified” result means the message demonstrably comes from the sender's domain. It doesn't mean the content, links or attachments are safe, or that the sender is trustworthy. A “Not protected” result doesn't necessarily mean a message is malicious.
